Points + fee calculator

Fees are exact from Imperial's fee table. Imperial has not published how points convert from volume, so this shows your fees and eligible volume only.

Rules, from Imperial's docs

  • Trading fee: 0.01% (1 bp) of notional, on open and on close. No platform fee, no spread markup.

    "Trading fee 0.01% (1 bp) of notional, on open and on close" · docs.imperial.space/trading/fees

  • Builder fee: up to 0.10% per fill, only when you trade through a third-party app that sets one. Not included in the numbers above.

    "Up to 0.10%, only when trading through a third-party app that sets one" · docs.imperial.space/trading/fees

  • Each week has a fixed pool: 15,000 points for trading and 5,000 for referrals. Your trading share comes from your volume that week.

    "15,000 for trading and 5,000 for referrals" · docs.imperial.space/rewards/points

  • Eligible volume is capped at 250x your average open interest, so churn without holding positions earns no extra.

    "Eligible volume is capped at 250x your average open interest" · docs.imperial.space/rewards/points

  • Boosts: Telegram linked, +0.50x for 7 days. Signing up with a referral code, +0.25x for 7 days. A boosted referral code, +0.50x for 30 days.

    Boosts table · docs.imperial.space/rewards/points#boosts

  • Early access: you need an invite code to open positions. Every trader gets 3 codes to start, and each invitee who trades $1,000 of positions earns you one more.

    "Every trader gets 3 codes to start" · docs.imperial.space/rewards/referrals-and-invites

Assumption in this tool: each open and each close counts as trading volume toward the weekly split. Imperial's docs do not say how volume is counted, so check that before you plan around it.

What this assumes

  • Isolated margin, the position's own collateral (collateral = size ÷ leverage). Liquidation is when equity falls below maintenance margin, the market's rate times notional.

    "A margin account is liquidated when its equity falls below its maintenance margin, measured at the mark price" · docs.imperial.space/trading/liquidation

  • Liquidation price here ignores fees and funding, and uses entry size for the maintenance rate. The real trigger sits a little closer to entry. Imperial's trade screen shows the exact price before you submit.

    "The trade screen shows your exact liquidation price" · docs.imperial.space/trading/margin

  • Maintenance rate per market comes from the API (mmr), and is fixed when you open a position.

    "Each market has its own maintenance rate. It is fixed when you open the position" · docs.imperial.space/trading/margin

  • Funding: when the rate is positive, longs pay shorts; when negative, shorts pay longs. It is charged on entry size, and capped at 0.05% per hour either way.

    "The rate can't exceed 0.05% per hour in either direction" · docs.imperial.space/trading/funding-rates

  • Isolated liquidation forfeits the remaining collateral. Fees are 0.01% on open and on close.

    "Remaining collateral of the liquidated position" · docs.imperial.space/trading/fees

Funding cost assumes today's rate holds for the whole period. Rates move, so it is an estimate.
